# China routing playbook

Use China only when the vetted supplier record supports the requested production route. The current private network has approved evidence for apparel, fabric, trims, and accessories.

- Route: apparel OEM/ODM, fabric, trims, labels, and accessories where the individual supplier tags and type match the RFQ.
- Buyer fit: complex or multi-component briefs that need one of those evidenced routes.
- Price-sensitive briefs: treat this as a comparison candidate only. Do not claim a price advantage without a current supplier quote.
- Ops checks: confirm MOQ, lead time, certification, availability, and shipping terms with the supplier.
