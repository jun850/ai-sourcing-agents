How sourcing works during beta testing

Our payment and transaction infrastructure is still being prepared. For now, Atelix handles fabric requests directly, so you can get considered support while we build the platform.

  1. Tell us what you needContact Atelix with the fabric you are interested in, or share your sourcing brief.
  2. We confirm your requirementsWe will ask about composition, weight, colour, quantity, MOQ, timing and any certification needs.
  3. Review swatchesFabric swatches are available at a flat price of $4 per swatch. We will confirm the request and next steps with you.
  4. Move forward manuallyOnce the right material and order details are confirmed, we will coordinate the next step directly with you.

Getting started

What is Atelix?+

Atelix is a B2B marketplace for fabrics, yarns, trims, accessories and OEM services. During beta testing, fabric sourcing requests are handled manually by the Atelix team.

What does “verified supplier” mean?+

Supplier profiles are reviewed for identity, business information and listed production capability. Confirm certification scope and validity with the supplier.

How do I start a fabric sourcing request?+

Email Atelix with the fabric you are interested in, or describe what you need. We will follow up to understand your composition, weight, colour, quantity, MOQ, timing and certification requirements.

Can I order fabric swatches?+

Yes. Fabric swatches are available for a flat price of $4 per swatch. Contact Atelix with the materials you would like to review and your requirements.

Orders

How do I request a quote?+

Email Atelix with the product or service, quantity, specification, destination and timing. We will coordinate the request manually during beta testing.

Do you accept garment sampling and production?+

Yes. Atelix can work with the companies and OEM factories listed on the platform to support garment sampling and production. Browse available factories and use the contact option on a factory profile to begin the conversation directly.

What happens once garment sampling begins?+

Once sampling begins, Atelix will connect you directly with the factory. During beta testing, we can continue to help connect you with relevant mills, factories and other partners as your order develops.

When will I receive a quote?+

Quote timing depends on each factory’s SLA and availability. Our usual SLA is one to five business days. Atelix will draft your quote once pricing is available from all factories involved, including relevant material mills, garment factories and other production partners.

What should I confirm before I pay?+

Confirm the specification, price, quantity, MOQ, lead time, shipping terms and payment instructions.

Can I change or cancel an order?+

Ask the supplier as soon as possible. Availability depends on the agreed terms and production stage.

Payments & refunds

How are payments handled?+

Payments are handled manually after the order details are confirmed. During beta testing, Atelix accepts bank transfer, Wise, or PayPal while it prepares its payment and transaction infrastructure.

How do I confirm a payment?+

Email the order number, payment method, transfer or payment date, amount, currency and receipt.

How do refunds work?+

Refund requests may be considered only when there is a mistake from the supplier. There are no refunds for a change of mind or mistakes made by the brand owner. Atelix will help the brand owner and supplier review eligible refund requests case by case. Email your order number and supporting information so we can assist.

Shipping & delivery

Who arranges shipment?+

The supplier arranges shipment and delivery. Confirm Incoterms, carrier, freight cost, dispatch timing and tracking information before payment.

Who handles customs and import duties?+

Responsibilities depend on the shipping terms agreed with the supplier.

Support & conflicts

What if I have a question or a conflict?+

Email Atelix with the order number and relevant supporting information.

Can Atelix help with a quality or delivery concern?+

Yes. Email the order number, photos and supporting information.
