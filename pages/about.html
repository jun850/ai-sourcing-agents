Built for the decisions behind every collection.

Fashion sourcing is often fragmented across messages, spreadsheets, catalogs and supplier conversations. Atelix is building a more organized path: discover what is available, compare the details that matter, and connect with the right production partner.

Product composition, weight, MOQ, lead time and supplier provenance remain visible so teams can evaluate options on facts. Athelia, our sourcing copilot, helps translate briefs, tech packs and fabric images into relevant catalog recommendations.

From an idea to a sourcing decision

Atelix keeps the process focused on usable information and the next practical step.

  1. 1

    Describe what you need

    Search the catalog, ask Athelia, or upload a tech pack or fabric reference.

  2. 2

    Compare viable options

    Review specifications, MOQ, pricing, supplier location and verification details.

  3. 3

    Move sourcing forward

    Shortlist products, contact suppliers, request quotes and place orders from one workspace.

Trust should be visible, not assumed.

Atelix reviews supplier identity, business information and listed production capability before applying verified status. Product and factory pages keep relevant sourcing facts close to the decision.

Verification is a starting point, not a substitute for due diligence. Buyers should confirm certification scope, samples, commercial terms and production requirements directly with each supplier.
