Getting started

What Atelix is built to help you do.

What is Atelix?+

Atelix is a B2B fashion sourcing marketplace for fabrics, yarns, trims, accessories and full-package OEM services. It helps brand owners compare the operating details that matter before requesting a quote.

What does “verified supplier” mean?+

Supplier profiles are reviewed for identity, business information and listed production capability. Certifications shown on a profile are supplier-held claims; confirm their scope and current validity directly with the supplier before placing an order.

Can I contact a supplier before ordering?+

Yes. Use the supplier contact path on the product or factory profile to discuss materials, quantities, sampling, colour, lead time and shipping terms before you commit.

Orders & supplier communication

Keep the commercial details explicit before production begins.

How do I request a quote?+

Start from a product or supplier profile and share the material or service, target quantity, specification, delivery destination and timing. The supplier will confirm what they can offer.

What should I confirm before I pay?+

Confirm the product or service specification, unit price, total quantity, MOQ, lead time, shipping terms, destination, payment instructions and any sample or approval requirement. Put changes in writing with the supplier.

Can I change or cancel an order?+

Ask the supplier as soon as possible. Whether a change or cancellation is available depends on the agreed terms and the production stage. Contact Atelix if you need help clarifying the next step.

Payments & refunds

Manual handling in the current Atelix MVP.

How are payments handled?+

Atelix currently handles payments by manual bank transfer. Once the order details are confirmed, follow the agreed payment instructions and retain the transfer receipt or proof of payment.

How do I confirm a bank transfer?+

Send the transfer confirmation through the agreed order communication channel. Include the order number, transfer date, amount, currency and receipt so the supplier and Atelix team can identify the payment quickly.

How do refunds work?+

Refunds are handled manually, not automatically. Contact Atelix with your order number and supporting information. We will coordinate with the supplier to review the issue and agree the appropriate next step under the confirmed order terms.

Shipping & delivery

Suppliers manage fulfilment directly.

Who arranges shipment?+

The supplier arranges shipment and delivery directly. Confirm the applicable Incoterms, carrier, freight cost, dispatch timing, customs responsibilities and delivery destination before payment.

When will I receive tracking information?+

The supplier shares tracking or dispatch information when it is available for the selected shipping method. Timing depends on the agreed lead time, production status and carrier arrangement.

Who handles customs and import duties?+

Responsibilities depend on the shipping terms agreed with the supplier. Confirm these responsibilities before you place the order, especially for cross-border delivery.

Support & conflicts

Help for both brand owners and suppliers, from question to resolution.

What if I have a question or a conflict?+

Contact Atelix as early as possible. Share the order number, a concise description, relevant messages, photos or documents, and the outcome you are seeking. Our team helps brand owners and suppliers work through questions, confusion and conflicts end to end.

Can Atelix help with a quality or delivery concern?+

Yes. Document the concern clearly and contact us with the relevant order information. We will help coordinate the conversation with the supplier and clarify the available next steps under the agreed order terms.

How do I contact the Atelix team?+

Email the team with your order number or sourcing brief. Include as much context as possible so we can route the request to the right person without delay.

Still need help?

For an order, include the order number. For sourcing help, include the material or service, target quantity and timing.
