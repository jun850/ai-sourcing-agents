What can I source on Atelix?

Fabrics, yarns, trims, accessories and full-package OEM services. Product pages surface composition, GSM, MOQ and indicative lead time so briefs start with the operating details.

Where are Atelix suppliers based?

The current verified supplier roster is based in China and Japan, including Zhejiang, Guangdong, Fukui, Wakayama, Hyogo, Ehime, Osaka and Tokyo.

How do I assess a supplier?

Review the factory profile, product specifications, supplier-held certifications and production capabilities, then request a quote for your material, quantity and timing.
