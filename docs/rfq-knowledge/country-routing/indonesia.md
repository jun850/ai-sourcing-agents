# Indonesia routing playbook

Use Indonesia only when the vetted supplier record supports the requested production route. The current private network includes approved apparel and beauty/personal-care capability records.

- Route: beauty or personal-care OEM/private-label work and the apparel capabilities evidenced on the selected supplier record.
- Buyer fit: buyers whose brief matches those evidenced capabilities; an Indonesian buyer location is only a soft internal tie-breaker.
- Price-sensitive briefs: do not infer a price advantage from country. Compare reviewed supplier quotes.
- Ops checks: confirm formula/compliance scope, MOQ, lead time, and current commercial terms.
