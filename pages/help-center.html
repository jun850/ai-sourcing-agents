Getting started

What is Atelix?+

Atelix is a B2B marketplace for fabrics, yarns, trims, accessories and OEM services.

What does “verified supplier” mean?+

Supplier profiles are reviewed for identity, business information and listed production capability. Confirm certification scope and validity with the supplier.

Can I contact a supplier before ordering?+

Yes. Use Email supplier on a product or factory profile.

Orders

How do I request a quote?+

Email the supplier with the product or service, quantity, specification, destination and timing.

What should I confirm before I pay?+

Confirm the specification, price, quantity, MOQ, lead time, shipping terms and payment instructions.

Can I change or cancel an order?+

Ask the supplier as soon as possible. Availability depends on the agreed terms and production stage.

Payments & refunds

How are payments handled?+

Payments are handled by manual bank transfer after the order details are confirmed.

How do I confirm a bank transfer?+

Email the order number, transfer date, amount, currency and receipt.

How do refunds work?+

Refunds are handled manually. Email Atelix with your order number and supporting information.

Shipping & delivery

Who arranges shipment?+

The supplier arranges shipment and delivery. Confirm Incoterms, carrier, freight cost, dispatch timing and tracking information before payment.

Who handles customs and import duties?+

Responsibilities depend on the shipping terms agreed with the supplier.

Support & conflicts

What if I have a question or a conflict?+

Email Atelix with the order number and relevant supporting information.

Can Atelix help with a quality or delivery concern?+

Yes. Email the order number, photos and supporting information.
