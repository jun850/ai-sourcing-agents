# A useful sourcing brief

Tell us what you need in your own words. A reference image or tech pack is welcome.

## Finished goods
"I need 500 blank heavyweight cotton tees for delivery to Australia in October. Black and white, sizes S-XL."

## Private or white label
"I need a private-label women's woven shirt based on this reference, 300 units, with our neck label and packaging. Please target delivery in November."

## CMT
"I need CMT for 800 woven trousers. We have a graded pattern and will supply fabric; this is for a bulk run after sample approval."

## Fabric, leather, and trims
"I need 2,000 metres of organic cotton twill, 250 gsm, 150 cm wide, for trousers."
"I need 10,000 matte black 18 mm recycled-polyester buttons for shirts."

If you do not know every detail yet, send what you have. Atelix will ask one useful follow-up at a time.
