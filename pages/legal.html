Atelix policies

Clear rules for better sourcing.

These policies explain how Atelix works during our marketplace MVP. They were last updated on 12 August 2026.

Terms & Conditions

Atelix is a B2B sourcing marketplace that helps brand owners discover suppliers, request quotes, and coordinate sourcing. We are not the manufacturer, seller, carrier, or importer of record unless we expressly agree otherwise in writing.

Accounts and platform use

Use accurate business information, keep account credentials secure, and use the platform only for legitimate business sourcing. Do not misrepresent your identity, products, authority, certifications, or commercial terms.

Listings, quotes and orders

Product information, availability, pricing, minimum order quantities, lead times and samples may change. A listing or quote is not a binding supply agreement until the supplier confirms the final scope, price, specification, payment terms and delivery terms. During the MVP, transactions and sourcing coordination may be handled manually.

Payments, refunds and disputes

Current payment methods are bank transfer, Wise and PayPal. Refunds may be considered only where there is a supplier error and remain subject to the supplier’s policy and the facts of the case. A change of mind or a brand-owner error is not a valid refund reason. Atelix can help both parties review a request, but does not guarantee an outcome.

Acceptable use and liability

Do not use Atelix for unlawful, fraudulent, infringing, unsafe, or deceptive activity. To the extent permitted by law, the service and marketplace information are provided as available. You remain responsible for your own supplier diligence, specifications, quality control, contracts, payments, import/export compliance and commercial decisions.

For a material order, confirm every production, quality, logistics and payment term directly with the supplier before sending funds or starting production.

Privacy Policy

We collect the information needed to run Atelix: account and business details, sourcing requests, communications, order-related information, device and usage data, and files you choose to upload.

How we use information

  • Provide accounts, sourcing support and marketplace features.
  • Connect relevant brand owners and suppliers where you request it.
  • Protect accounts, prevent fraud and improve the service.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to valid requests.

Sharing and retention

We share information only with service providers, suppliers or brand owners involved in your requested sourcing activity, or where required by law. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, dispute handling, security and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable law and necessary record retention. Contact info@arcanawangsa.com. Our service may link to third-party sites; their privacy practices are their own.

Trust & Safety

Atelix aims to make B2B sourcing more transparent, not to replace professional verification. We review supplier information and may show verification signals, but brand owners should independently validate critical commercial and compliance matters.

What we expect

  • Suppliers: accurate listings, legitimate certifications, realistic availability and honest production capability.
  • Brand owners: clear briefs, accurate specifications, timely communication and responsible payment decisions.
  • Everyone: respectful conduct and prompt reporting of suspected fraud, counterfeit goods, unsafe products or intellectual-property concerns.

Report a concern

Email info@arcanawangsa.com with relevant links, order details and supporting evidence. We may restrict accounts, remove content or assist parties with a case review where appropriate.

Policy changes

We may update these policies as Atelix develops. The current version is posted here; continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.
