Why this list is different
"We make for Peloton — buy direct at 90% off." You've seen the videos. Most of them are trading
companies borrowing a famous name. Here's what almost nobody tells you: real supplier relationships
are on the public record. Chinese factories listed on stock exchanges must disclose their major
customers in filings. Brands publish supplier lists. We read those documents — in Chinese — so you don't
have to.



     Where this comes from
     This directory is one piece of the China Insider Sourcing knowledge library — research we produce for our
     members, released publicly. The written half — the guides, the contracts, the templates — fits in documents like
     this one. The other half can't be written down: a room of importers comparing real quotes, and experts from
     both sides of the trade answering in real time, at every step of your sourcing journey. Which of these suppliers
     answers a small buyer — and how to write the first message so they do — is what that room works out every day.
     Both halves live at chinainsider.news/sourcing.



Every claim in this book carries a source tag:


       OFFICIAL       Disclosed by the factory or the brand itself: stock-exchange filings, IPO prospectuses, annual reports,
     published supplier lists, or official ownership records.

       REPORTED         Documented by major business media or securities research. Solid, but one step removed.

     If a claim couldn't clear one of these two bars, it didn't make the book.




     What this directory is — and is not
        • It is not a place to buy branded goods cheap. You cannot buy a Peloton from Peloton's factory — contracts
          forbid it, and anyone offering "brand overstock" is selling fakes.
        • It is a map of proven capability. The play: hire the same factory — or its industrial cluster — to build your own
          product, under your own brand, at factory economics.
        • Big fish need big buyers. Some giants here only take enterprise orders. We list them anyway — as intelligence
          and as the quality ceiling — and rate each factory's friendliness to smaller buyers:

     ●●●         Actively serves small/mid buyers — B2B storefront, published terms, realistic MOQs ●● ● Realistic for
     mid-size orders or via a trading arm ● ●● Enterprise-scale — listed for intelligence

Fitness & Recovery
The home-fitness boom was assembled in China — including the bike with the cult following. These are
the factories on the record.



Lixuan Sports 力玄运动
NINGBO & SHANGHAI · EXERCISE BIKES, ROWERS, TREADMILLS · SSE IPO FILINGS

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL       Peloton — disclosed in Lixuan's own IPO registration documents as its largest customer during the home-
fitness boom, at one point the majority of its revenue.       REPORTED       Nautilus/Bowflex-tier programs among other
overseas fitness brands.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Connected exercise bikes, rowers, ellipticals and treadmills at brand-program quality — the actual answer to "who
builds the bike everyone pays $44/month to ride."

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Canton Fair sporting-goods halls.

●● ● Program-scale OEM, but China's fitness belt (Ningbo–Xiamen–Qingdao) quotes mid-size orders — start with the
cluster below.



Impulse Health Tech 英派斯
QINGDAO, SHANDONG · GYM EQUIPMENT · SZSE: 002899

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Listed gym-equipment maker; filings show a large export OEM/ODM book serving overseas fitness brands
and commercial-gym chains alongside its own Impulse brand.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Commercial-grade strength machines, cardio lines, home racks and benches — the "feels like the gym" tier of home
fitness.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International · IWF Shanghai / FIBO.

●● ● ODM catalogs available; commercial-quality private label is the sweet spot.



Rotai Health 荣泰健康
SHANGHAI · MASSAGE CHAIRS & RECOVERY DEVICES · SSE: 603579

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        One of the world's largest massage-chair manufacturers per its listing filings, with a substantial export
OEM business alongside its own ROTAI brand.         REPORTED      OEM programs for major Asian and Western wellness
brands.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Full-body massage chairs, massage guns, compact recovery devices — the "recovery" half of the fitness aisle, where
margins live.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Canton Fair · CES (exhibits internationally).

●● ● Big-ticket OEM; compact recovery devices open the door for smaller brands.

Outdoor & Camping
The tents, mats and sleeping bags of the world's biggest outdoor retailers come disproportionately from
three listed Chinese specialists — all of which name their customers in filings.



Mobi Garden 牧高笛
NINGBO, ZHEJIANG · TENTS & CAMPING GEAR · SSE: 603908

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL       Decathlon — disclosed across Mobi Garden's listing documents and annual reports as its anchor OEM
customer for tents. The company runs both the OEM book and its own fast-growing Mobi Garden brand.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Tents from festival-cheap to alpine-grade, shelters, camp furniture. If you've slept in a Decathlon tent, decent odds it
was born here.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International · ISPO / OutDoor shows.

●● ● OEM-native; mid-size private-label programs realistic.



Zhejiang Natural 浙江自然
TIANTAI, ZHEJIANG · INFLATABLE OUTDOOR GEAR · SSE: 605080

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Its listing prospectus discloses Decathlon and Sea to Summit among top customers for TPU inflatable
sleeping mats and outdoor gear.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Self-inflating and air sleeping pads, waterproof bags, inflatable water gear — the TPU-lamination specialist of the
industry.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International.

●● ● Narrow specialist, deep capability — bring a spec sheet.



Yangzhou Jinquan 扬州金泉
YANGZHOU, JIANGSU · SLEEPING BAGS, TENTS, OUTDOOR APPAREL · SSE: 603307

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Listing filings disclose leading European and North American outdoor brands and retailers as its principal
OEM customers, with sleeping bags as the anchor category.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Down and synthetic sleeping bags at every temperature rating, plus tents and outdoor apparel — one of the largest
sleeping-bag OEM bases anywhere.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International · OutDoor by ISPO.

●● ● Category specialist; private-label sleeping bags are its bread and butter.

Apparel — The Niches
Volume 1 covered the garment giants behind Lululemon and Nike. This chapter is the pieces everyone
wears and nobody thinks about — socks, zippers, dress shirts — each ruled by a listed specialist.



Jasan Group 健盛集团
HANGZHOU, ZHEJIANG · SOCKS & SEAMLESS KNITWEAR · SSE: 603558

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        One of the world's largest sock OEMs per its filings, with plants in China and Vietnam.   REPORTED   Puma,
Under Armour and major US sock brands documented among its customer base; its seamless division serves
international intimates and activewear labels.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Performance socks at hundreds of millions of pairs a year, plus seamless underwear and activewear.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International.

●●●        Socks are the classic first apparel product: low MOQ per SKU, forgiving sizing, subscription economics.



Weixing 伟星股份
LINHAI, ZHEJIANG · ZIPPERS, BUTTONS, TRIMS · SZSE: 002003

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        By its own filings, one of the world's largest zipper and button makers — the biggest challenger to Japan's
YKK.    REPORTED         Its SAB zippers run through the supply chains of major global apparel brands.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Zippers, buttons, snaps and trims — half your wardrobe closes with hardware from one of two companies, and this is
the Chinese one.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site (SAB) · sales offices across apparel clusters.

●●●        Not a product to sell — a component to spec. Naming SAB or YKK in your garment spec is a one-line quality
upgrade.



Luthai Textile 鲁泰纺织
ZIBO, SHANDONG · SHIRTING FABRIC & DRESS SHIRTS · SZSE: 000726

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        The world's largest producer of high-end yarn-dyed shirting fabric per its filings, vertically integrated from
cotton to finished shirt.         REPORTED   Documented as a longtime shirt maker for premium international menswear
labels.

WHAT THEY MAKE

The fabric and the finished dress shirts behind a large share of premium menswear — the quiet monopoly of the
category.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · fabric shows (Intertextile Shanghai).

●● ● Fabric minimums are accessible; finished-shirt programs need real volume.

Home & Sleep
The chair you sit on, the desk you raise, the mattress that arrived in a box — three listed companies, three
on-the-record client books.



Henglin Home 恒林股份
ANJI, ZHEJIANG · OFFICE & GAMING CHAIRS · SSE: 603661

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL       IKEA — disclosed across Henglin's annual reports as a principal customer, alongside major global furniture
retailers (Nitori among documented programs).

WHAT THEY MAKE

Office chairs, gaming chairs, sofas and desks from the world's chair capital — Anji county, which produces roughly half
the planet's swivel chairs.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International · Canton Fair furniture phase.

●● ● Retailer-scale OEM, but Anji's cluster below it quotes containers, not truckloads.



Loctek Ergonomic 乐歌股份
NINGBO, ZHEJIANG · STANDING DESKS & ERGONOMICS · SZSE: 300729

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Here's the twist: FlexiSpot — the standing-desk brand all over your feed — is Loctek's own brand, per its
filings. The "Western ergonomics company" is a Ningbo manufacturer that went direct.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Height-adjustable desks, monitor arms, ergonomic accessories — plus the cross-border logistics arm it built to deliver
them.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International.

●● ● ODM desks available to brand — you'd be selling next to their own flagship, price accordingly.



Mlily / Healthcare Co. 梦百合
NANTONG, JIANGSU · MEMORY-FOAM MATTRESSES · SSE: 603313

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Filings show a majority-export OEM book supplying major US and European mattress and furniture brands,
with plants in China, the US, Serbia, Spain and Thailand. Global partner of Manchester United under its own MLILY brand.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Memory-foam mattresses, pillows and bed bases — much of the "mattress-in-a-box" wave was compressed and
rolled on lines like these.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Interzum / sleep-industry shows · Alibaba International.

●● ● Private-label programs standard; US warehousing simplifies logistics for American sellers.



    C L U S T E R I N T E L Chairs: Anji (安吉). Mattresses & home textiles: Nantong (南通) — also the world's bedding-fabric capital.
    Standing desks: Ningbo. On 1688: 电动升降桌 OEM (electric standing desk + OEM).

Tools & Floorcare
The hardware aisle and the vacuum aisle are two of retail's most concentrated Chinese supply chains —
and the three companies below put their customer lists in their filings.



Great Star Industrial 巨星科技
HANGZHOU, ZHEJIANG · HAND TOOLS & STORAGE · SZSE: 002444

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Asia's largest hand-tool maker; its annual reports name the major North American home-improvement
and mass retailers — Lowe's and Walmart among them — as core customers. Also owns the US brands Arrow Fastener,
Shop-Vac and SK Tools outright.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Hand tools, tool storage, laser measures — a large share of the private-label tool wall at American retail.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Canton Fair hardware halls · National Hardware Show.

●● ● Retail-program scale; its home cluster (Hangzhou–Yongkang) quotes smaller tool programs readily.



Ningbo Dechang 德昌股份
NINGBO, ZHEJIANG · VACUUM CLEANERS & MOTORS · SSE: 605555

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Its listing prospectus discloses TTI — the group behind Hoover and Dirt Devil — as its dominant customer,
with SharkNinja also documented among major clients. One factory, most of a vacuum aisle.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Complete vacuum cleaners and the high-speed motors inside them; expanding into garden tools and personal-care
motors.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Canton Fair.

● ●● Anchor-customer OEM — listed as intelligence: this is who actually builds "American" vacuums.



Chervon Holdings 泉峰控股
NANJING, JIANGSU · POWER TOOLS & OPE · HKEX: 2285

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Per its listing prospectus: a two-decade ODM history for the world's top tool brands — Bosch and Stanley
Black & Decker among disclosed customers — before building its own brands EGO (battery outdoor equipment) and
FLEX, and buying SKIL from Bosch.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Cordless power tools and outdoor power equipment — EGO's battery platform is the category leader that came from
an ODM.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site (enterprise programs).

● ●● The proof of the whole thesis: yesterday's contract manufacturer is today's #1 brand. Study the path.
