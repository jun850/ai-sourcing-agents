Two kinds of truth in this book

craze is doing marketing, not manufacturing. So this volume tells you two kinds of truth, clearly labeled:

    Vinyl / resin figures (搪胶 — soft vinyl): Dongguan's designer-toy belt — the same district ecosystem that grew

Squishies & Sensory — 捏捏
Hyperrealistic bread you can crush. Butter you can squeeze forever. The 捏捏 ("nie-nie" — squeeze-
squeeze) craze is a pure cluster category: no brands, no filings, thousands of workshops — and a
genuinely serious safety angle most sellers discover too late.


    CLUSTER MAP — NO BRAND FACTORIES EXIST                                         AND THAT'S THE HONEST ANSWER


    Slow-rising PU squishies (hyperrealistic food, bread, desserts — 慢回弹, "slow rebound"): Shantou–Chenghai's
    foam-toy lines and Yiwu's novelty belt. Judge makers by mold sharpness and scent control — the realism is the
    product.

    Butter squishies (黄油捏捏) — the soft-silicone, hand-finished tier that took over Chinese social media before
    crossing west: born in Taobao workshops, now scaled by the silicone factories of Dongguan and Shenzhen.
    Genuinely different material class from PU — silicone versions are more durable, more expensive, and easier to
    make safe.

    The trend clock: this category peaked hard in China first — textbook 西渡 ("crossing west") material. By the time
    it's all over your feed, the Chinese supply chain has already standardized: dozens of makers, falling prices, ready
    molds. Good for sourcing, bad for being early.

       1688 → 捏捏乐 慢回弹 (squishy + slow-rising) · 硅胶捏捏 定制 (silicone squishy + custom) · 仿真食物 解压 (realistic food +
       stress-relief)




     The safety trap that ends stores
     Squishies sit at the intersection of three regulatory tripwires: they look like food (small children bite them),
     they're scented (fragrance chemistry), and they're soft plastic (plasticizers). The EU has recalled squishy
     products over chemical emissions; toy rules (EN71 / ASTM F963 + CPC) apply the moment your listing photos
     show a child. The defense is boring and absolute: buy from makers holding test reports in their own name for the
     exact material, spec "food-look products: non-toxic, phthalate-free, low-VOC" in writing, and decide up front
     whether you're selling a toy (full kids' compliance) or an adult desk item (market it strictly that way). The cheap
     workshop that can't produce a report isn't cheap — it's a recall you haven't had yet. Members run exactly these
     checks past the room — reports, specs, quotes — before wiring anything.

     The red line: cosmetic peptides ≠ "research peptides"
     Everything above is topical, cosmetic-grade material. The other peptide economy on your feed — injectable
     "research peptides" sold for human use — is unapproved-drug territory: illegal to market for human consumption
     in the US and most markets, and outside anything this directory covers. A supplier who blurs that line casually
     will blur every other line too. Cosmetic formulations still need their own compliance (MoCRA registration in the
     US; CPNP + Responsible Person in the EU) — real ODMs handle this paperwork daily.

Scalp Care & Hair Growth
Rosemary oil, scalp serums, hair-growth gummies — one of the highest-margin corners of beauty, and a
category with no famous factories at all. It runs on one city.


    CLUSTER MAP                                                                           GUANGZHOU IS THE ANSWER


    Guangzhou — Baiyun district 白云区 — is the manufacturing capital of Chinese beauty: by industry counts, a third
    or more of China's licensed cosmetics factories operate in this one city, with haircare ODMs concentrated in
    Baiyun and Huadu. Everything from scalp serums to rosemary oil to sulfate-free systems is formulated, filled and
    boxed here, mostly by companies you'll never see named on a bottle.

    How to buy it: this is ODM country — pick an existing tested formula, customize scent/packaging/actives, put your
    brand on it. Licensed factories carry a 化妆品生产许可证 (cosmetics production license) — ask for the license
    number and verify it; it's the two-minute check that separates a real plant from a filling workshop.

       1688 → 洗发水 OEM 广州 (shampoo + OEM + Guangzhou) · 头皮精华 代加工 (scalp serum + contract manufacturing)




     The claims trap
     "Hair growth" is a drug claim, not a marketing flourish. In the US, growth claims put you in FDA drug territory
     (minoxidil is an OTC drug for a reason); in the EU and China, growth/anti-hair-loss products face special
     registration. The compliant lane — where nearly the whole viral category actually lives — is cosmetic claims:
     "supports a healthy scalp," "reduces breakage," "thicker-looking hair." Write your claims before you brief the
     factory, not after the labels are printed. A good Guangzhou ODM will flag this unprompted; one who promises
     "growth guaranteed" formulas is selling you a warning letter.

Shapewear & Seamless
The Skims economy. Another no-brand-factories category — but with the most geographically
concentrated supply chain in this book.


    CLUSTER MAP                                                                         THREE TOWNS, ONE INDUSTRY


    Gurao, Shantou 谷饶 — the underwear town: by trade-press counts, a staggering share of the world's bras and
    shapewear basics come from this single Guangdong town and its neighbor Chendian 陈店. Volume tier: lowest
    prices, MOQs from a few hundred pieces, quality you must specify hard. Yanbu, Foshan 盐步 — the quality tier:
    China's premium bra-engineering cluster, where the domestic high-end brands were born. Yiwu & Zhuji —
    seamless knitting (the Skims-style one-piece construction) on Italian Santoni-class circular machines.

    What decides quality here: fabric composition (spec nylon/spandex percentages in writing — composition fraud
    is this category's signature move), compression zoning (ask which zones and what pressure), and sizing
    discipline (demand their size chart against measurements, then measure the samples — see our Cluster Map
    guide's apparel entry for the full checklist). For the engineering ceiling of this category, see Regina Miracle in Vol. 1
    — the listed giant behind big-brand bras.

       1688 → 塑身衣 OEM (shapewear + OEM) · 无缝内衣 义乌 (seamless underwear + Yiwu) · 文胸 盐步 (bras + Yanbu)
