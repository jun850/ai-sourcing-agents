Small categories, one-product towns
The categories in this volume share a shape: modest MOQs, high margins, passionate niche buyers — and
supply chains concentrated in single Chinese towns most people have never heard of. A town in Henan
that grows diamonds. A town in Shandong that makes most of the world's false lashes. A town in Zhejiang
where pens outnumber people ten thousand to one. This is where small brands are born.

Stationery & Journaling
The journaling economy — planners, washi tape, fountain pens, sticker books — is a small-brand paradise:
light, unregulated, high-margin, endlessly customizable. Its supply chain runs through one export
specialist and one astonishing town.



Guangbo Group 广博股份
NINGBO, ZHEJIANG · PAPER STATIONERY & OFFICE SUPPLIES · SZSE: 002103

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        A listed Ningbo stationery maker built on export OEM — notebooks, planners, albums and paper goods —
with overseas retail and brand customers as the core of its filings-documented business.                       REPORTED       Longtime
supplier into major US retail stationery programs.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Notebooks and journals in every binding, photo albums, folders, paper gift items — the private-label engine behind a
lot of Western shelf stationery.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Alibaba International · Canton Fair stationery halls · Paperworld.

●●●        Export-OEM native; custom journals at accessible MOQs are exactly its lane.



    THE TOWN                                                            FENSHUI — WHERE THE WORLD'S PENS COME FROM


    Fenshui, Tonglu county, Zhejiang 分水镇 — "China's pen town": one town of tens of thousands of people producing
    pens by the tens of billions a year across hundreds of factories and workshops. Ballpoints, gels, fountain pens,
    novelty pens — if it writes, someone in Fenshui makes it, brands it, and ships it, at MOQs that start in the hundreds.
    Washi tape & stickers: the Dongguan–Shenzhen printing belt plus Yiwu. The giant for scale: M&G 晨光 (SSE:
    603899) makes more pens than any company on earth — study its catalog as the quality ceiling, then buy your
    actual first order from the town.

       1688 → 中性笔 分水 (gel pen + Fenshui) · 笔记本 定制 (notebook + custom) · 和纸胶带 印刷 (washi tape + printing)




    S M A L L - B R A N D N O T E Stationery is the classic first product: no certifications, light freight, forgiving quality bar. The one
    trap is print quality on customs — always approve a printed proof (打样, a pre-production sample) before the run, never
    a screen mockup.

Candles & Home Fragrance
The Etsy-to-empire category. Most Western candle brands pour domestically and source everything else
— vessels, wicks, fragrance, gift boxes — from three Chinese clusters. Or skip the pouring too: China's
candle industry has a verified anchor.



Kingking 青岛金王
QINGDAO, SHANDONG · CANDLES & HOME FRAGRANCE · SZSE: 002094

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        One of the world's largest candle manufacturers and exporters per its listing history — a decades-long
OEM supplier of candles and home-fragrance products into major Western retail programs.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Scented candles, tealights, pillars, wax melts and fragrance products at retail-program scale and quality.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Canton Fair · Alibaba International.

●● ● Retail-program OEM; mid-size private-label runs realistic. Below it, Hebei and Qingdao's candle belt quotes
smaller.



    THE COMPONENT MAP                                                        WHAT CANDLE BRANDS ACTUALLY IMPORT


    Glass vessels: Xuzhou, Jiangsu 徐州 — China's glass-bottle capital; candle jars, lids and gift-set glass at MOQs
    from a few thousand pieces, custom colors and embossing cheap at volume. Fragrance oils: the Guangzhou
    fragrance belt — spec IFRA-compliant oils in writing, with the documentation to prove it. Wicks, tins, rigid gift
    boxes: Yiwu and the Dongguan print belt. Many "handmade in [your country]" candle brands are exactly this
    stack plus local wax and labor.

       1688 → 蜡烛杯 徐州 (candle jar + Xuzhou) · 香薰蜡烛 OEM (scented candle + OEM) · 香精 IFRA (fragrance oil + IFRA)




     The safety line
     Candles burn houses when done wrong. Spec and test: wick-to-vessel sizing (an oversized wick in a narrow jar
     overheats the glass), flash-point documentation on fragrance oils, and heat-resistant glass for containers. US
     sellers: follow ASTM candle standards and label per NCA guidance. A vessel supplier who can't discuss annealing
     and thermal shock is a homeware supplier, not a candle-jar supplier.

Pearls & Lab-Grown Diamonds
Two jewelry revolutions, two Chinese towns: the lake town that grows most of the world's freshwater pearls,
and the county in Henan that grows diamonds in machines. One has a verified listed anchor; both are
open to small buyers.



Power Diamond 力量钻石
ZHECHENG, HENAN · LAB-GROWN DIAMONDS · SZSE: 301071

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        A listed producer of cultivated diamonds — industrial and jewelry-grade — from Zhecheng county 柘城, the
capital of China's lab-diamond industry, which by trade counts grows the majority of the world's HPHT diamonds.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Rough and polished lab-grown diamonds, diamond micro-powder — the upstream of the engagement-ring
disruption.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate/B2B channels; loose stones flow to small buyers via the Zhecheng and Shenzhen trade networks below.

●● ● Upstream producer; small jewelry brands buy certified loose stones downstream — insist on IGI/GIA lab-grown
certificates per stone.



    THE TOWNS                                                                  WHERE JEWELRY ACTUALLY STARTS


    Shanxiahu, Zhuji, Zhejiang 山下湖 — the freshwater-pearl capital of the world: by trade counts the overwhelming
    majority of global freshwater pearls pass through this one lake town, now famous for livestream pearl-opening
    sales. Loose pearls, strands and finished pieces at every grade; tiny minimums. Shuibei, Shenzhen 水贝 — China's
    gold-and-jewelry wholesale district: finished jewelry, settings, and the fastest place to turn stones into product.
    Panyu, Guangzhou 番禺 — the fine-jewelry manufacturing belt where global brands have their pieces made.

    The grading trap: pearls and lab stones are graded goods sold by photo. Defense: buy graded lots against
    written standards (pearl luster/surface/roundness; diamond 4Cs with lab certificates), sample the exact grade,
    and expect the bulk to match the sample — the Guide 03 playbook applies to gems too.

       1688 → 淡水珍珠 山下湖 (freshwater pearl + Shanxiahu) · 培育钻石 裸钻 (lab diamond + loose stone) · 925银 首饰 定制
       (S925 jewelry + custom)

Lashes & Press-On Nails
Beauty's smallest products, biggest margins — and two of the most concentrated supply chains on earth.
No famous factories; two towns instead.


    CLUSTER MAP — NO BRAND FACTORIES EXIST                                                     THE TOWNS ARE THE BRAND


    Pingdu, Qingdao, Shandong 平度 — the false-eyelash capital: by trade counts, on the order of seven in ten of the
    world's false lashes come from this one district's thousands of workshops and factories. Strip lashes, clusters,
    magnetic, mink-look synthetics — every construction, every band, at MOQs from a few hundred pairs with
    custom packaging. The quality tiers are real: band flexibility, hair softness and consistency separate a US$0.30
    pair from a US$1.20 pair — sample three tiers and feel it.

    Press-on nails (穿戴甲) — the category China's own social commerce industrialized first: hand-painted and
    printed sets from workshops across Shandong, Guangdong and Yiwu, plus salon-grade custom sizing. Same 西渡
    ("crossing west") pattern as always: China's platforms saturated first; the ready supply chain is your inheritance.

    Compliance note: lash adhesive and nail glue are the regulated part — cyanoacrylate formulations need proper
    labeling and, in the EU, CPNP notification. Buy glue from suppliers with safety documentation; the lashes
    themselves are the easy half.

       1688 → 假睫毛 平度 (false lashes + Pingdu) · 穿戴甲 定制 (press-on nails + custom) · 睫毛胶水 检测报告 (lash glue + test
       report)




    S M A L L - B R A N D N O T E Lashes are arguably the single most accessible beauty product to brand: sub-US$1,000 first orders,
    light air freight, Instagram-native. The moat is curation and packaging, not access — which is why packaging suppliers
    (Dongguan print belt) matter as much as the lash town. Members launching in categories like this get their first quotes
    and packaging specs checked in the room before paying.

Board Games & Card Printing
Nearly every Kickstarter board game you've backed was printed in China — and unusually for this book,
the publishers say so openly, crediting their manufacturers by name on campaign pages and in
rulebooks. That makes this niche's small factories among the best-documented anywhere.



LongPack Games 龙包印务
SHANGHAI · BOARD-GAME MANUFACTURING · PRIVATE

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        Credited openly and repeatedly by Western game publishers on their own campaign pages and in
published rulebooks as their manufacturer — the board-game world's equivalent of a brand-published supplier list. A
fixture of tabletop-industry press.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Complete board games: cards, boards, custom dice and miniatures, inserts, retail-ready boxes — one-stop from print
files to shrink-wrapped pallets.

HOW TO REACH

Direct via its site — publishes quoting processes for indie publishers; exhibits at Essen Spiel and GAMA.

●●●        Built for exactly this buyer: first-time publishers with a 1,000–3,000-copy print run.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Card games, board games, custom components; strong on card stock quality and finishes — the details TCG-
adjacent buyers obsess over.

HOW TO REACH

●●●        Indie-friendly quoting; bring a print-ready file and a component list.



    C L U S T E R I N T E L The wider Shenzhen–Dongguan print belt handles everything adjacent: tarot and oracle decks,

    the dice and minis, not just the box.

Wigs & Hair Extensions
A multi-billion-dollar category almost entirely invisible to people outside it — and by trade counts, around
half the world's wigs ship from one inland Chinese city.



Rebecca 瑞贝卡
XUCHANG, HENAN · WIGS & HAIR PRODUCTS · SSE: 600439

VERIFIED CLIENTS

 OFFICIAL        The world's largest wig maker per its filings — the listed anchor of Xuchang 许昌, exporting human-hair and
synthetic products across North America, Africa and Europe under OEM programs and its own brands.

WHAT THEY MAKE

Lace-front wigs, extensions, braids, toppers — from synthetic fashion pieces to premium human-hair units.

HOW TO REACH

Corporate site · Canton Fair · Alibaba International.

●● ● Program-scale OEM up top; the Xuchang cluster below it serves small buyers daily.



    THE TOWN                                                                                 XUCHANG — THE HAIR CAPITAL


    Xuchang's hair trade is centuries old and now fully e-commerce native: thousands of factories and workshops, a
    cross-border seller ecosystem shipping direct to US and African consumers, and MOQs that start at literally one
    unit (dropship) up through container programs. What decides quality: hair origin and processing (virgin vs
    processed — demand honesty in writing), lace quality (Swiss vs film), density and construction consistency.
    Sample the exact SKU; "same as the video" is not a spec.

       1688 → 假发 许昌 (wigs + Xuchang) · 真人发 蕾丝头套 (human hair + lace wig)
